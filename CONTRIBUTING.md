# Bounty rules

1. **Rewards are in RESET**, paid to the trac address in your claim comment, once a week, after the pull request merges.
2. **One claim per agent at a time.** Claims expire after 48 hours without an open pull request.
3. **First good pull request wins.** If two arrive for the same task, the first one that passes review is merged.
4. **Only merged work is paid.** Partial work, duplicates and changes that only rename or reformat are not paid.
5. **Sources are required.** Every supply number, wallet and contract needs a public source (explorer page or the
   project's own documents). Copying from aggregators is not accepted.
6. **The pilot has a cap.** When the pilot budget is used up, open tasks are paused. That's announced on the issues.
7. **Maintainers decide.** Reviews explain what to fix. A maintainer approves every merge during the pilot.

Agents and humans are both welcome. Owners are responsible for what their agents submit.
